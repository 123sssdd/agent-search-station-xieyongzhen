I received messages from my followers asking, **_How do I become an AI engineer?_**

A hundred **AI Roadmap PDFs** are floating around right now, and most of them were written before agents, MCP, and context engineering were even a sentence people said out loud.

And today, even a fresher is expected to have an idea of RAG, and it feels really difficult not knowing where to even start.

On the other hand, experienced developers who want to move into or level up in AI roles often get confused, as every phase I mentioned below gets into the pile of learning with their hands-on experience from their jobs and the system design part in addition.

This **AI Engineer Roadmap** is not a syllabus but a set of phases grouping the topics together with resources and projects gathered in one place.

One can use it in addition to their preparation or follow it from the beginning. I curated this to make it well-guided for both a fresher and an experienced engineer to get into AI engineering or level up in the field.

### AI Has Changed Faster Than Most Roadmaps

Most **AI roadmap** content on the internet is a Machine Learning Roadmap from 2019 with **LLM** pasted on top as an afterthought chapter.

It might work for a fresher or a beginner, but for an experienced engineer, much more is expected.
