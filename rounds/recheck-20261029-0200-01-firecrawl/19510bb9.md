Last week, I was helping a senior dev who was struggling with implementing a simple chatbot using agents and MCP. He’d been watching tutorials for weeks but couldn’t connect the dots. That’s when I stumbled upon Hugging Face courses which are COMPLETELY FREE. I went through few of the courses and damn, the quality is too good for beginners as well as intermediate devs. Here are 5 courses that I believe you should enroll in this summer.

## 1) Large Language Models (LLM) Course

**Link —** [**https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/chapter1/1**](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/chapter1/1)

Remember the first time you used ChatGPT and thought, “How the heck does this thing understand me?” Well, this course demystifies that magic. This course doesn’t just teach you to use them, it also teaches you to understand them.

**Pro Tip:** Start with smaller models first. I made the mistake of trying to run a 70B parameter model on my laptop. Let’s just say my laptop wasn’t happy about it!

## 2) Deep Reinforcement Learning

**Link —** [**https://huggingface.co/learn/deep-rl-course/en/unit0/introduction**](https://huggingface.co/learn/deep-rl-course/en/unit0/introduction)

Remember playing video games and dying 100 times before beating a level? That’s basically what we’re teaching AI to do, except it learns way faster than us. At the end of this course, you’ll get a solid foundation from the basics to the SOTA (state-of-the-art) of methods.

## 3) Diffusion Models Course

**Link —** [**https://huggingface.co/learn/diffusion-course/en/unit0/1**](https://huggingface.co/learn/diffusion-course/en/unit0/1)

Okay, confession time. I’m terrible at drawing. But with diffusion models, I’m creating art that looks great.

**Beyond Pretty Pictures:** Sure, generating “cyberpunk cats” is fun (I may have generated 100+ variations), but the real power is in:

- Product design mockups
- Architectural visualizations
- Educational content creation
